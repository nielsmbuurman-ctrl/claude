# Carfo: website plan & script

**Formula:** the clean look of Meller, with the checkout math of Fresh Supps.
Show all ten frames on one quiet white page. Make every extra pair so cheap that
buying one feels like a mistake. Let creators carry the traffic.

**One line for the whole site:** *Ten frames. €19.95 each. Every extra pair €15.*

### Decisions locked

| | Decision |
|---|---|
| Brand | **Carfo** (new brand) |
| Price | **€19.95 per frame** |
| Platform | **Shopify, with our own custom theme** |
| Launch | **All 10 frames at once** |

## 1. What the two sites do

### Fresh Supps (the funnel)

A creator-led supplement brand. Founded 2023 by Greg Helton. Bought in 2026 by
fitness creator Alex Eubank (about 1.3M YouTube subscribers, nearly 3M TikTok
followers) and relaunched in May 2026 at lower prices.

| What they do | Verified detail | What it means for Carfo |
|---|---|---|
| **Tiny catalog, variety in the variants** | Four core products (Pre, Creatine/HMB, Creatine Mono, Amino) plus merch. Pre comes in 8 flavours. | Ten frames are our "flavours". Show all ten everywhere. No deep menus. |
| **Build your Bundle** | A dedicated bundles page: pick 2 Pack, 3 Pack or 4 Pack, mix products, "Free Shipping Unlocked". | **The core mechanic to copy.** Any 2, 3 or 4 frames, every extra pair €15. |
| **Subscribe & Save 15%** | On product pages. | Glasses are not consumable. The cheap extra pair and a drop list replace it. |
| **Short, punchy promise** | "Real doses and flavors that actually hit." "Built for lifters who show up, ask questions, and care what goes in their body." | One-line claims, each backed by a spec (UV400, mm sizes, case included). |
| **Affiliate program ("Join Team Fresh")** | Free to join, no minimum following, 20% cash commission, a custom 10% follower code, 25% personal discount, affiliate-only contests (cash, gear, team retreats). Runs on Superfiliate with personal landing pages. | Copy the structure. Lower the rates to fit a €19.95 product (section 6.9). |
| **Price as news** | Relaunch prices 15–25% lower (Pre $42.99, Amino $32.99). Relaunch announced with a date. | Launch on a set date, with a waitlist. |
| **Reviews on product pages** | e.g. 4.81/5 from 74 reviews on one product. | Get real reviews early through seeding. Never fake them. |
| **Growth order** | Founder audience first, then affiliates and community, then paid ads (TikTok, Amazon). | Same order for Carfo: creators and affiliates first, paid ads once the numbers allow it. |
| **Wholesale page** | A wholesale application page. | Later: festival shops, boutiques, beach clubs. |

### Meller (the look)

Barcelona, founded 2014. €28.3M revenue in 2024. Lenskart bought 80% in July
2025 for about €41.5M. One of the clearest examples of a digital-first sunglasses
brand.

| What they do | Verified detail | What it means for Carfo |
|---|---|---|
| **Price sweet spot** | €49–79 in Europe, $65–99 in the US. Hero styles at $65. | Carfo looks like Meller at under half the price. That gap is the pitch. |
| **2-for mechanic** | A combo page (1 pair $65, 2 pairs $75) and repeated 2x1 events with codes (e.g. "12th Anniversary 2x1", 30 Apr–1 Jun 2026; codes like FLASH2X1, SUMMER). Codes do not stack. Analysts call the 2x1 a built-in viral loop: one pair for you, one for a friend. | **Make it permanent and automatic.** No codes. |
| **Free shipping threshold** | Free shipping over €55. 2–3 days across Europe, 24–48h express to BE/FR/DE. | Our threshold is €25, so it kicks in at two pairs. |
| **Returns** | 30 days. The customer pays return postage unless the item is faulty (the EU site gives a prepaid label). | **We beat it: free returns, label by email.** |
| **Lifetime warranty** | Covers solder points that fail through manufacturing faults, not scratches or breakage. | Only promise what the frames can survive. |
| **Newsletter** | 10% off the first order via a popup and the footer form. | Use a free gift instead, so it does not eat into the bundle price. |
| **Lens spec up front** | UV400 on everything. TAC polarized lenses on the polarized line. | Lens spec on every card and product page. |
| **Navigation by shape and material** | New In, Slim, Bold, Polarized, Acetate, Bio-based, Premium, Chains, Blue Light, Meller Lab, Virtual Try-On, Find Your Frame. | With 10 frames, use **filter chips** instead of a menu: Slim · Bold · Tinted · Clear. |
| **Naming** | Invented model name + colourway: "Kessie All Black", "Nayah Fog Olive", "Bashira Salt Kakao". | Every Carfo frame gets a name and a character. |
| **Design language** | Press describes "minimalist lines, geometric frames and a clean aesthetic" with a Mediterranean feel. | Section 7. |
| **Try-on** | Instagram AR try-on filters, AR ads, a Virtual Try-On collection. Reviewers still name fit risk as a reason not to buy. | Millimetre sizes and a fit scale first. Try-on later. |
| **Marketing engine** | 1,000+ micro-influencer collaborations in a single year. UGC in ads lowered cost-per-click. About 750K Instagram followers. Each collection is run like a series, with every frame cast as a character (the "Miss Galaxy" campaign). Festival and club roots: club nights, a drop treasure hunt at Sónar. | Seed micro-creators, run UGC, treat each frame as a character. |

**Meller's weak spots, from Trustpilot (4.4/5, 6,000+ reviews).** Customers love
the style and price. The complaints repeat: return hassle and return postage,
frames that feel "small, tight or narrow", and some delivery delays. **Carfo's
answer:** free returns, millimetre sizes on every frame, fast shipping from the
Netherlands.

## 3. The offer

### Price ladder

| Option | Price | Per pair | You save | Shipping |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Single** | €19.95 | €19.95 | — | €3.95 |
| **The Pair** (any 2) | €34.95 | €17.48 | €4.95 | Free |
| **The Crew** (any 3) | €49.95 | €16.65 | €9.90 | Free + free cord |
| Any 4 | €64.95 | €16.24 | €14.85 | Free + free cord |

**The message everywhere: "€19.95. Every extra pair €15."** Easy to remember,
easy to say in a 10-second video, and it scales to any number of pairs.

- **Free shipping from €25.** One pair (€19.95), even with a cord (€24.90), stays
  under it. Two pairs always clear it. The €3.95 shipping fee is the nudge
  toward a second pair.
- **Why €15 and not €10 for the extra pair:** at €10, a Pair order earns less
  than a single pair (see below). At €15, every extra pair adds profit.

### Margin check (illustrative: replace with your real costs)

Assumed: frame landed €5.00 · case + cloth €1.20 per pair · box €0.80 per order ·
postage €4.50 for 1–2 pairs (letterbox parcel), €6.95 for 3+ · payment fee €0.40 ·
cord €1.00 · NL VAT 21%.
**Free returns:** 8% of orders come back. A returned order costs outbound
postage + a €4.50 return label + box + payment fee; frames and cases go back to
stock; the whole order is refunded, shipping fee included (EU rule).

| Order | Revenue ex VAT | Costs | Profit if kept | **Expected profit after returns** |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Single (incl. €3.95 shipping) | €19.75 | €11.90 | €7.85 (40%) | **€6.41** |
| The Pair | €28.88 | €18.10 | €10.78 (37%) | **€9.11** |
| The Crew (incl. cord) | €41.28 | €27.75 | €13.53 (33%) | **€11.44** |
| The Pair via an affiliate (10% code, 10% commission) | €26.00 | €18.10 + €2.60 | €5.30 (20%) | **€4.06** |

**What this means for Carfo:**

1. **The bundle take rate is the business.** A Pair earns about €2.70 more than
   a single pair. The share of orders with 2+ pairs is the number to watch.
2. **Paid ads are hard on the first order.** To break even you can pay about €6
   to win a single-pair order, or €9 for a Pair. So launch on creators,
   affiliates and organic, and only scale ads that beat those numbers.
3. **Postage is the cost to attack.** It is €4.50 of the €18.10 on a Pair, and
   another €4.50 on every return. A shipping contract (e.g. through Sendcloud or
   a PostNL business account) and a box that fits through the letterbox with two
   cases will move profit more than any design choice.
4. **Keep the affiliate rates modest.** Fresh's 20% commission is built for a
   $40 product. At €19.95, 10% + 10% still leaves profit (section 6.9).

### Returns policy (free returns)

- **30 days, free.** The customer asks for a return online and gets a prepaid
  label by email. Refund within [5] working days of receipt, to the original
  payment method.
- **To swap:** return it for free and order the frame you want. We ship the new
  order right away; we don't wait for the return to arrive.
- **Returning part of a set:** the remaining pairs keep their set price. Example:
  return one pair from The Pair and you get **€15** back (you keep one pair at
  €19.95). Return the whole set and you get everything back. Put this rule in
  the terms and on the returns page.
- Frames must be unworn, in the case, with the cloth.

### Trust stack (shown on every product page and in the cart)

- **UV400 lenses, CE marked, filter category printed on every product page.**
- **Hard case and cloth with every pair.**
- **30-day free returns.**
- **[1-year warranty on hinges and frame]** (only if your supplier backs it).

## 4. The 10 frames

### Give every frame a job

All ten launch at once, so the mix has to work from day one.

| Slots | Role | Pick frames that are | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1–2 | **Heroes** | Flattering on most faces, photograph well | They carry the hero image and the first ads |
| 3–5 | **Everyday core** | Black, tortoise, clear: safe choices | They make the second pair an easy decision |
| 6–8 | **Statement** | Tinted lenses, wraps, shields, Y2K | They drive UGC and comments ("which number?") |
| 9–10 | **Wildcards** | Your two most distinctive frames | Talking points for creators; the frames people screenshot |

**Balance check:** at least 3 that suit wide faces, at least 3 that suit narrow
faces, at least 2 clear or light frames, no more than 2 wraps or shields.

### The Carfo ten

**How the names work.** Each frame gets a short, warm-sounding name from Spanish,
Italian or Portuguese that carries the meaning of its supplier name. So the name
still fits once the details are confirmed. Every name starts with a different
letter (A, B, C, F, L, M, N, P, S, Z), so comments ("Fiera or Sombra?"), SKUs
and the size table never mix two frames up. None of them matches a Meller or
pep-schep model name I came across.

Format on the site: `Name, Frame / Lens`.

## 7. Visual style guide

The verified part: Meller is described as minimalist, geometric and clean, with a
Mediterranean feel, and it puts the product first. The specifics below are my
interpretation of that style for Carfo, not measured from their site.

### Wordmark

`CARFO` in uppercase, set in the brand sans with wide tracking (+12–16%). No icon
at launch. A favicon is the `C` alone.

### Colour

| Token | Value | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `--bg` | `#FFFFFF` | Page |
| `--tile` | `#F2F1EE` | Packshot background (must match the photo backdrop) |
| `--ink` | `#111111` | Text, buttons |
| `--mute` | `#6B6B6B` | Secondary text |
| `--line` | `#E4E2DD` | Dividers |
| `--accent` | `#FF5A1F` (or a tint from one of the ten lenses) | Badges and the progress bar only |

One accent, used sparingly. The lens tints are the colour; the site stays neutral.

### Type

- **One sans serif family**, two weights (e.g. *Inter Tight* or *Archivo*,
  self-hosted in the theme).
- Headlines: 500–600 weight, tight tracking, short (max ~6 words).
- Labels and buttons: uppercase, 12–13 px, +6% letter-spacing.
- Body: 15–16 px, 1.5 line height.
- Prices in tabular figures.

### Layout

- Grid: 4 columns desktop, 2 mobile, 16 px gutters, 16 px side margin on mobile.
- Product tiles 4:5. No borders, no shadows, no rounded cards.
- Buttons: solid black, square corners (or 2 px), 48 px high, full width on mobile.
- Generous white space between sections (96–128 px desktop, 64 px mobile).
- Never more than one call to action per section.

### Motion

- Image swap on hover: 200 ms crossfade. Drawer: 250 ms slide.
- No parallax or scroll-jacking on shop pages. Motion stays in the hero and the loupe.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.

### Copy voice

Meller's restraint with Fresh's punch. Short. Confident. A bit cheeky. Every
claim has a spec behind it. No exclamation marks.

## 10. Traffic

### Phase 1: creators (Meller's engine, at Carfo's scale)

- **30 micro-creators** (5k–50k followers) in Dutch fashion, festival and city
  life. Meller ran 1,000+ collaborations a year; we start with 30 and keep the
  ones that sell.
- Send each one **The Pair**: one for them, one to give away. The parcel *is*
  the offer, so it shows up in their content.
- Every creator gets an affiliate code (section 6.9). Track sales per code.
- Cost per creator: two pairs, box and postage, about €18 at the assumed costs.

### Phase 2: affiliates (Fresh's engine)

- Open `Join the crew` to everyone at launch. No minimum following.
- Monthly contest: top 3 sellers win frames and cash.
- Give affiliates a content kit: photos, three hooks, the price line.

### Phase 3: paid ads (only when they beat the margin)

At €19.95 the first-order budget is tight (about €9 per Pair order, section 3).
So start by **boosting creator posts that already perform** (Meta partnership
ads, TikTok Spark Ads) rather than making ads from scratch. Meller found UGC
beats polished brand content on cost per click. Three starter scripts:

**A. "The second pair" (15 s)**
- 0–2 s: friend hands friend a pair. *"I got you sunglasses."* / *"Why?"*
- 2–6 s: *"Because the second pair was fifteen euros."*
- 6–12 s: quick cuts of both wearing them, outdoors.
- 12–15 s, on screen: `€19.95 · every extra pair €15 · carfo.[nl]`

**B. "Ten frames, one face" (12 s)**
- One face, ten frames, a cut on every beat.
- On screen: `Which number? 👇` (drives comments).

**C. "Through the lens" (10 s)**
- Point-of-view shot: the same festival scene seen through four tints.

## 11. The Carfo Shopify theme

A custom Online Store 2.0 theme, built only for ten products and this funnel.
Small, fast, and fully editable in the theme editor.

### Principles

- **Sections everywhere.** Every block on every page is a section with
  settings, so copy and images can change without code.
- **No framework, no jQuery.** Small web components in plain JavaScript.
  Budget: JS under 30 KB and CSS under 40 KB (gzipped), LCP under 2.5 s on
  mobile.
- **One place for the price ladder.** Theme settings hold `single_price`,
  `extra_pair_price` and `free_shipping_threshold`. Every price shown on the
  site is calculated from them. The discount app (below) is set to the same
  numbers. Launch checklist item: theme settings and discount app match.
- **Product data lives in metafields**, not in descriptions, so the size table,
  chips, quiz and safety block build themselves.
- **NL first**, English second (`locales/nl.default.json`, `en.json`).
- **Accessible:** real buttons, focus states, alt text from metafields,
  keyboard-usable drawer and quiz, colour contrast AA.

### File structure

```
layout/      theme.liquid · password.liquid
config/      settings_schema.json (price ladder, colours, announcement)
locales/     nl.default.json · en.json
templates/   index.json · collection.json · product.json · cart.json · search.json · 404.json
             page.json · page.build-your-set.json · page.find-your-frame.json
             page.size-and-fit.json · page.join-the-crew.json · password.json · customers/*
sections/    announcement-bar · header · footer · hero · proof-bar · product-grid
             bundle-tiers · lens-loupe · quiz-teaser · quality · ugc-wall · reviews
             story · faq · email-capture · main-product · main-collection
             build-your-set · find-your-frame · size-table · crew-page · cart-drawer
snippets/    product-card · price-ladder · fit-chip · lens-chip · bundle-selector
             cart-progress · safety-info · icon
assets/      base.css · carfo.js (web components) · fonts
```

### Components (carfo.js)

| Component | Does |
|---|---|
| `<cart-drawer>` | Opens on add, re-renders through the Section Rendering API, shows the progress line from section 6.6 |
| `<bundle-selector>` | Tier radio cards on the product page, the "pick your second pair" row, live button price, adds all picked frames in one `/cart/add.js` call |
| `<set-builder>` | The Build-your-set page: slots, grid, sticky bar |
| `<frame-quiz>` | Three questions → two products, rules set in the theme editor (section blocks) |
| `<lens-loupe>` | The see-through-the-lens effect, with a static fallback for reduced motion |
| `<filter-chips>` | Client-side filtering of the ten cards (no page reload) |

Savings and totals in the drawer are read from the cart's own discount fields
(`line_level_discount_allocations`, `cart_level_discount_applications`), never
calculated only in the browser, so the drawer always matches checkout.

### Metafields (namespace `carfo`)

`one_liner` · `fit` (narrow/medium/wide) · `frame_width_mm` · `lens_width_mm` ·
`bridge_mm` · `temple_mm` · `filter_category` · `uv400` · `polarized` ·
`material` · `weight_g` · `in_the_box` · `wear_to` (list) · `tint_hex` ·
`shape` (slim/bold/tinted/clear) · `manufacturer` (metaobject: name, address,
contact; used for the safety block)

### What the theme can't do alone (apps)

| Need | Why an app | Option |
|---|---|---|
| **"Any 2 / 3 / 4" set pricing** | Themes can't change prices. A custom discount Function would do it, but custom apps with Functions need Shopify Plus. On other plans, use a public App Store discount app. | A mix-and-match / volume discount app that supports "any items from a collection, fixed price per quantity" |
| Post-purchase one-click offer | Only apps can add post-purchase pages | e.g. ReConvert, AfterSell |
| Reviews with photos | Collection and moderation | Judge.me (shown through its app block in our section) |
| Email / SMS | Flows | Klaviyo, or Shopify Email to start |
| Affiliates | Codes, links, payouts | Shopify Collabs or UpPromote to start |
| Free return labels | Return portal + labels | e.g. Sendcloud (Dutch, return portal) |
| Analytics | Pixels with consent | Shopify's Google & YouTube, Meta and TikTok apps |

Check each app's price and Functions support before installing.

### Development workflow

- Build in this repo under `carfo/theme/` while we design. For the live store,
  move the theme into its **own repository with the theme at the root**:
  Shopify's GitHub integration only accepts the standard theme folder
  structure on the connected branch.
- `main` branch connected to the live theme. Feature branches → preview themes.
- Local preview with `shopify theme dev`; lint with `shopify theme check` on
  every push.
- Before launch: test checkout end to end (single, Pair, Crew, 4 pairs,
  affiliate code, return), on iPhone and Android.

## 12. Legal and compliance

Check these with your supplier and an advisor. This is a checklist, not legal advice.

- **Trademark and domain.** Check "Carfo" in the Benelux (BOIP) and EU (EUIPO)
  registers before printing it on frames or cases. Secure the domain and social
  handles.
- **CE marking for sunglasses.** In the EU, sunglasses count as personal
  protective equipment and must meet EN ISO 12312-1. Each frame needs a CE mark,
  a filter category (0–4) and a user leaflet. Ask your supplier for the EU
  Declaration of Conformity for every frame. **Category 4 lenses are not allowed
  for driving.** Say so on the product page.
- **GPSR (EU General Product Safety Regulation).** Online product pages must show
  the manufacturer (or the EU responsible person) with an address, a product
  identifier, and any warnings.
- **Only claim what is tested.** "UV400" and "polarized" need test reports.
- **Right of withdrawal.** 14 days is the legal minimum; Carfo gives 30. Include
  the model withdrawal form. On a withdrawal you must also refund the standard
  outbound shipping fee.
- **Partial returns of sets.** State the "remaining pairs keep their set price"
  rule (section 3) in the terms before launch.
- **Price display (Omnibus rule).** A struck-through "was" price must be the
  lowest price of the last 30 days. Set savings shown against the €19.95 single
  price are fine.
- **Company details.** Dutch web shops must show their KvK number, VAT number,
  address and email.
- **Cookies.** A consent banner that blocks tracking until the visitor agrees.
- **Influencers.** Seeded and affiliate content must be labelled as an ad or
  partnership (Dutch Advertising Code / Reclamecode Social Media).
