# Website plan & script: a 10-frame sunglasses launch

**Formula:** the clean look of Meller, with the checkout math of Fresh Supps.
Show all ten frames on one quiet white page. Make the second pair so cheap that
buying one feels like a mistake. Let creators carry the traffic.

> **Research note.** freshsupps.com and mellerbrand.com are both blocked by this
> environment's network policy, so I could not load the pages or screenshot them.
> Everything under "Verified" comes from search-indexed pages, press coverage and
> review sites (sources at the bottom). Anything I inferred is marked as inferred.
> Prices, shipping times and legal points are things you must check before launch.
> Screenshots of either site would let me tighten the visual sections.

> **Assumption.** This repo already holds the PEP SCHEP shades shop (7 models), so
> I assume the 10 pairs are a sunglasses line sold to Dutch shoppers. The plan
> works for a brand-new name too: swap `[BRAND]` and `[CITY]`.

## 1. What the two sites do

### Fresh Supps (the funnel)

A creator-led supplement brand. Founded 2023 by Greg Helton. Bought in 2026 by
fitness creator Alex Eubank (about 1.3M YouTube subscribers, nearly 3M TikTok
followers) and relaunched in May 2026 at lower prices.

| What they do | Verified detail | What it means for glasses |
|---|---|---|
| **Tiny catalog, variety in the variants** | Four core products (Pre, Creatine/HMB, Creatine Mono, Amino) plus merch. Pre comes in 8 flavours. | Ten frames are our "flavours". Show all ten everywhere. No deep menus. |
| **Build your Bundle** | A dedicated bundles page: pick 2 Pack, 3 Pack or 4 Pack, mix products, "Free Shipping Unlocked". | **This is the core mechanic to copy.** Any 2 or 3 frames at a set price. |
| **Subscribe & Save 15%** | On product pages. | Glasses are not consumable. Swap it for a cheap second pair and a drop list. |
| **Short, punchy promise** | "Real doses and flavors that actually hit." "Built for lifters who show up, ask questions, and care what goes in their body." | One-line claims, each backed by a spec (UV400, mm sizes, case included). |
| **Affiliate program ("Join Team Fresh")** | Free to join, no minimum following, 20% cash commission, a custom 10% follower code, 25% personal discount, affiliate-only contests (cash, gear, team retreats). Runs on Superfiliate with personal landing pages. | Copy this almost 1:1. It is the cheapest traffic you will get. |
| **Price as news** | Relaunch prices 15–25% lower (Pre $42.99, Amino $32.99). Relaunch announced with a date. | Launch on a set date, with a waitlist and a launch price. |
| **Reviews on product pages** | e.g. 4.81/5 from 74 reviews on one product. | Get real reviews early through seeding. Never fake them. |
| **Growth order** | Founder audience first, then affiliates and community, then paid ads (TikTok, Amazon). The founder planned to step back over time. | Same order for us: creators and affiliates first, paid ads once the site converts. |
| **Wholesale page** | A wholesale application page. | Later: festival shops, boutiques, beach clubs. |

### Meller (the look)

Barcelona, founded 2014. €28.3M revenue in 2024. Lenskart bought 80% in July
2025 for about €41.5M. One of the clearest examples of a digital-first sunglasses
brand.

| What they do | Verified detail | What it means for us |
|---|---|---|
| **Price sweet spot** | €49–79 in Europe, $65–99 in the US. Hero styles at $65. They sit between fast fashion and €200+ designer frames. | Pick one clear price and keep it everywhere. Ours is in section 3. |
| **2-for mechanic** | A combo page (1 pair $65, 2 pairs $75) and repeated 2x1 events with codes (e.g. "12th Anniversary 2x1", 30 Apr–1 Jun 2026; codes like FLASH2X1, SUMMER). Codes do not stack. Analysts call the 2x1 a built-in viral loop: one pair for you, one for a friend. | **Make the 2-for permanent and built into the price.** No codes needed. |
| **Free shipping threshold** | Free shipping over €55. 2–3 days across Europe, 24–48h express to BE/FR/DE. | Set the threshold just above one pair so it kicks in at two. |
| **Returns** | 30 days. The customer pays return postage unless the item is faulty (the EU site gives a prepaid label). | Meet or beat this. See below. |
| **Lifetime warranty** | Covers solder points that fail through manufacturing faults, not scratches or breakage. | Only promise what your frames can survive. A 1-year warranty is honest and still rare at this price. |
| **Newsletter** | 10% off the first order via a popup and the footer form. | Use a free gift instead, so it does not eat into the bundle price (section 9). |
| **Lens spec up front** | UV400 on everything. TAC polarized lenses on the polarized line. | Put the lens spec on every card and product page. |
| **Navigation by shape and material** | New In, Slim, Bold, Polarized, Acetate, Bio-based, Premium, Chains, Blue Light, Meller Lab, Virtual Try-On, Find Your Frame. | With 10 frames, use **filter chips** instead of a menu: Slim · Bold · Tinted · Clear. |
| **Naming** | Invented model name + colourway: "Kessie All Black", "Nayah Fog Olive", "Bashira Salt Kakao". | Every frame gets a name and a character. "Calora, Clear / Ice Blue". |
| **Design language** | Press describes "minimalist lines, geometric frames and a clean aesthetic" with a Mediterranean feel. | Section 7. |
| **Try-on** | Instagram AR try-on filters, AR ads, a Virtual Try-On collection. Reviewers still name fit risk as a reason not to buy. | Start with millimetre sizes and a fit scale. Add try-on later (section 11). |
| **Marketing engine** | 1,000+ micro-influencer collaborations in a single year. UGC in ads lowered cost-per-click. About 750K Instagram followers. Each collection is run like a series, with every frame cast as a character (the "Miss Galaxy" campaign). Festival and club roots: club nights, a drop treasure hunt at Sónar. | Seed micro-creators, run UGC ads, and treat each frame as a character. |

**Meller's weak spots, from Trustpilot (4.4/5, 6,000+ reviews).** Customers love
the style and price. The complaints repeat: return hassle and return postage,
frames that feel "small, tight or narrow", and some delivery delays. **These are
our openings:** list millimetre sizes on every frame, offer free exchanges, and
ship fast from the Netherlands.

## 2. What we take, what we skip

| Take from Fresh | Take from Meller | Skip |
|---|---|---|
| Build-your-bundle with "free shipping unlocked" | White, quiet, product-first layout | Subscribe & save (glasses are not consumable) |
| Affiliate program terms and contests | Model name + colourway naming | Discount codes for the core deal (bake it into the price) |
| Short claims, each backed by a spec | Lens spec and warranty up front | A big menu tree (10 frames do not need one) |
| A dated launch with a waitlist | 2-for as a social, viral mechanic | Heavy scroll animation on shop pages |
| Creators first, paid ads later | Character-led campaigns, festival roots | A 10% popup that stacks on bundle prices |

**The site in one sentence:** *Ten frames on one clean page, and the second pair costs twenty euros.*

## 3. The offer

### Price ladder (recommended)

| Option | Price | Per pair | You save | Shipping |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Single** | €29.95 | €29.95 | — | €3.95 |
| **The Pair** (any 2) | €49.95 | €24.98 | €9.95 | Free |
| **The Crew** (any 3) | €64.95 | €21.65 | €24.90 | Free + free cord |

Why these numbers:

- **€29.95 single** stays under the €30 impulse line. It is well below Meller
  (€49+) and above throwaway festival frames (€10–20), which fits a clean brand.
- **"Second pair: €20. Third pair: €15."** That is the message on the cart, the
  product page and the thank-you page. It is easy to remember and easy to say in
  a 10-second video.
- **The free-shipping threshold is €35.** One pair never reaches it and two pairs
  always do, so the shipping fee becomes the nudge toward two. (A single pair
  plus two add-ons can cross it too; that is fine.)
- **The Crew is the anchor.** It makes The Pair look like the smart choice.
  In the illustrative numbers below, the third pair adds little profit. Its job
  is to make The Pair the middle option.

**If your landed cost is higher**, keep the shape and raise every tier: €34.95 /
€54.95 / €69.95. The cart line becomes "Second pair: €20. Third pair: €15."
again, so the message does not change.

### Margin check (illustrative: replace with your real costs)

Assumed costs: frame landed €6.00, case + cloth €1.20 per pair, box €0.80 per
order, postage €4.50 (1–2 pairs, letterbox parcel) or €6.95 (3 pairs), payment
fee €0.40, returns allowance 5% of revenue. NL VAT is 21%.

| | Revenue ex VAT | Costs | Contribution | Margin |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Single (incl. €3.95 shipping) | €28.01 | €14.14 | **€13.87** | 50% |
| The Pair | €41.28 | €22.16 | **€19.12** | 46% |
| The Crew (incl. €1 cord) | €53.68 | €33.43 | **€20.25** | 38% |

**What to take from it.** In this example the most you can spend to win an order
and still break even on that first order is about €14 for a single pair or €19
for The Pair. Every euro the second pair adds to the order raises what you can
pay for traffic. Redo this table with your supplier's prices before you fix the
ladder.

### Guarantees (the trust stack)

- **UV400 lenses, CE marked, filter category printed on every product page.**
- **Hard case and cloth with every pair.**
- **30 days to change your mind. Free exchange for another frame.** Meller's
  reviews show returns are where trust breaks. A free exchange keeps the money
  in the shop.
- **1-year warranty on hinges and frame** (only if your supplier backs it).

## 4. The 10 frames

### Give every frame a job

| Slots | Role | Pick frames that are | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1–2 | **Heroes** | Flattering on most faces, photograph well | They carry the ads and the hero image |
| 3–5 | **Everyday core** | Black, tortoise, clear: safe choices | They make the second pair an easy decision |
| 6–8 | **Statement** | Tinted lenses, wraps, shields, Y2K | They drive UGC and comments ("which number?") |
| 9–10 | **Drop 02** | Your most distinctive two | Option: launch with 8 and release these 4–6 weeks later, so you have news in week 6 |

**If this is the PEP SCHEP line:** the seven models already in `products.json`
fill seven slots. Rave Classic (thin black frame) and Calora (clear frame, light
blue lens) are natural heroes. Sinix (Y2K wrap) is a statement piece. Cristal
Gris, El Silencio, Lunares and Tecna fill the core once their details are
confirmed. Three slots stay open.

**Balance check for the ten:** at least 3 that suit wide faces, at least 3 that
suit narrow faces, at least 2 clear or light frames, no more than 2 wraps or
shields.

`MODEL, Frame / Lens`. Examples: **Calora, Clear / Ice Blue** and **Rave
Classic, Black / Smoke**. One invented name per frame and one line of character:
*"The fresh one. Goes with everything."*

## 5. Funnel map and sitemap

```
 TRAFFIC                     LAND                   DECIDE                    PAY                    KEEP
 Creators + affiliate codes  Home (all 10 visible)  Product page              Cart drawer            Thank-you offer
 UGC ads (Meta, TikTok)  ──▶ Collection "All 10" ─▶ + bundle selector     ──▶ "2nd pair €20" bar ──▶ "3rd pair €15, 1 click"
 Organic social              Quiz result            Build-your-set page       Checkout (iDEAL first) Email/SMS flows
 Email list (drops)          Affiliate landing                                                         Drop 02 → back to top
```

**The one rule of this funnel:** every step between landing and checkout shows
the next-pair price. Fresh does this with bundle tiers. Meller does it with 2x1.
We do it with "Second pair: €20".

## 7. Visual style guide

The verified part: Meller is described as minimalist, geometric and clean, with a
Mediterranean feel, and it puts the product first. The specifics below are my
interpretation of that style, not measured from their site.

### Colour

| Token | Value | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `--bg` | `#FFFFFF` | Page |
| `--tile` | `#F2F1EE` | Packshot background (must match the photo backdrop) |
| `--ink` | `#111111` | Text, buttons |
| `--mute` | `#6B6B6B` | Secondary text |
| `--line` | `#E4E2DD` | Dividers |
| `--accent` | `#FF5A1F` (pick one lens tint) | Badges and the progress bar only |

One accent, used sparingly. The lens tints are the colour; the site stays neutral.

### Type

- **One sans serif family**, two weights (e.g. *Inter Tight* or *Archivo* from Google Fonts).
- Headlines: 500–600 weight, tight tracking, short (max ~6 words).
- Labels and buttons: uppercase, 12–13 px, +6% letter-spacing.
- Body: 15–16 px, 1.5 line height.
- Prices in tabular figures.

### Layout

- Grid: 4 columns desktop, 2 mobile, 16 px gutters, 16 px side margin on mobile.
- Product tiles 4:5. No borders, no shadows, no rounded cards.
- Buttons: solid black, square corners (or 2 px), 48 px high, full width on mobile.
- Generous white space between sections (96–128 px desktop, 64 px mobile).
- Never more than one call to action per section.

### Motion

- Image swap on hover: 200 ms crossfade. Drawer: 250 ms slide.
- No parallax or scroll-jacking on shop pages. Keep the scroll animation for the
  hero and the loupe only.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.

### Copy voice

Meller's restraint with Fresh's punch. Short. Confident. A bit cheeky. Every
claim has a spec behind it. No exclamation marks except on drop day.

## 8. Photography shot list

**Per frame (6 images, all ten frames = 60):**

| # | Shot | Rules |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | Front packshot | Seamless `#F2F1EE` backdrop, the same lens height and scale in every shot (frame fills ~80% of width), soft shadow below |
| 02 | Three-quarter angle | 30° turn, same light |
| 03 | Side / temple | Shows hinge and arm |
| 04 | On-model A | Hard sun, shoulders up, neutral clothes |
| 05 | On-model B | A different face shape and gender from A, so people see the fit |
| 06 | Detail or in-case | Lens tint over a white card, or folded in the case |

**Campaign (10–15 images):** real places in hard sunlight: a beach (e.g.

**Honesty rule:** product photos must be real photos of the real frames.
Generated images (see `PROMPTS.md`) are fine for mood and backgrounds, not for
showing what a product looks like.

## 9. Email, SMS and popup

### Popup

- Shows after 15 s or 50% scroll, **not** on the first product page view, and
  never on mobile within the first 10 s.
- Offer: **a free cord with the first order** (or 10% off a single pair only).
  Do not give 10% off everything: it stacks on The Pair and cuts your margin.
- Ask for email first, then SMS on step 2 (optional, with clear consent).

## 10. Traffic

### Phase 1: creators (Meller's engine, at our scale)

- **30 micro-creators** (5k–50k followers) in Dutch fashion, festival and city
  life. Meller ran 1,000+ collaborations a year; we start with 30 and add the
  ones that sell.
- Send each one **The Pair**: one for them, one to give away. The parcel *is*
  the offer, so it shows up in their content.
- Every creator gets an affiliate code (section 6.9). Track sales per code.

### Phase 2: affiliates (Fresh's engine)

- Open `Join the crew` to everyone at launch. No minimum following.
- Monthly contest: top 3 sellers win the next drop and cash.
- Give affiliates a content kit: product photos, three hooks, the price line.

### Phase 3: paid ads (once the site converts at 2%+)

UGC-style ads on Meta and TikTok. Meller found UGC beats polished brand content
on cost per click. Three starter scripts:

**A. "The second pair" (15 s)**
- 0–2 s: friend hands friend a pair. *"I got you sunglasses."* / *"Why?"*
- 2–6 s: *"Because the second pair was twenty euros."*
- 6–12 s: quick cuts of both wearing them, outdoors.
- 12–15 s, on screen: `Any 2 for €49.95 · [brand].nl`

**B. "Ten frames, one face" (12 s)**
- One face, ten frames, a cut on every beat.
- On screen: `Which number? 👇` (drives comments).
- End card: `All ten → [brand].nl · any 2 for €49.95`

**C. "Through the lens" (10 s)**
- Point-of-view shot: the same festival scene seen through four tints.
- On screen: `Pick your tint.` End card as above.

### Calendar hooks (NL)

- **Black Friday / Sinterklaas / Christmas:** The Pair as a gift box.
- **Koningsdag (27 April):** an orange-lens frame or an orange cord.
- **Festival season (May–August):** main ad push, Drop 02.
- **Back to the city (September):** clear frames and everyday core.

## 12. Legal and compliance

Check these with your supplier and an advisor. This is a checklist, not legal advice.

- **CE marking for sunglasses.** In the EU, sunglasses count as personal
  protective equipment and must meet EN ISO 12312-1. Each frame needs a CE mark,
  a filter category (0–4) and a user leaflet. Ask your supplier for the EU
  Declaration of Conformity for every frame. **Category 4 lenses are not allowed
  for driving.** Say so on the product page.
- **GPSR (EU General Product Safety Regulation).** Online product pages must show
  the manufacturer (or the EU responsible person) with an address, a product
  identifier, and any warnings.
- **Only claim what is tested.** "UV400" and "polarized" need test reports.
- **Right of withdrawal.** Give 14 days by law (we offer 30) and include the
  model withdrawal form.
- **Price display (Omnibus rule).** A struck-through "was" price must be the
  lowest price of the last 30 days. Bundle savings shown against the current
  single price are fine.
- **Company details.** Dutch web shops must show their KvK number, VAT number,
  address and email.
- **Cookies.** A consent banner that blocks tracking until the visitor agrees.
- **Influencers.** Seeded and affiliate content must be labelled as an ad or
  partnership (Dutch Advertising Code / Reclamecode Social Media).

## 15. Decisions you need to make

1. **Brand:** is this the PEP SCHEP line, or a new name? (Shopify has a name and
   domain tool if you want ideas.)
2. **Price ladder:** €29.95 / €49.95 / €64.95, or the higher €34.95 ladder?
   Depends on your landed cost per frame.
3. **Platform:** standalone Shopify (recommended) or the existing WooCommerce shop?
4. **Launch all 10 now, or 8 + 2 as Drop 02?**
5. **Returns:** free exchange only, or free returns too? Free returns convert
   better and cost more.
6. **Affiliate rates:** 15% commission / 10% code / 30% personal discount, or
   closer to Fresh's 20%?
7. **Lenses:** any polarized frames? If yes, they can carry a higher price.
